Pilltide, the medicine reminder that doesn't get in the way

A free Android app for anyone who takes daily medicines or looks after someone who does. Reminders ring on the minute, and you answer them from the notification.

Get it on Google Play

Free. Offline. No account.

What you get

  • On-time reminders. Exact alarms, set again after a restart. A check warns you if your phone would block them.
  • Answer from the notification. Taken, Snooze 10 min or Skip. It asks again every 10 minutes until you answer.
  • Any schedule. Daily times, weekdays, every few days or hours, 21/7 cycles, or as needed.
  • Honest log. The real time and amount of every dose. Late, missed and skipped stay separate.
  • Progress and a doctor report. Streaks, 7- and 30-day rates, a 4-week calendar and a PDF.
  • Private. Your list stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Free, supported by ads

Ads never appear on a reminder, a notification or after you tap Taken. Pilltide is a reminder and log, not medical advice.
